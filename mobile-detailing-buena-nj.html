Car Care Notes for Buena Drivers

Buena is surrounded by farmland and back roads, so vehicles here pick up dust, mud and bug splatter in the warmer months and road salt in the winter. Left alone, bug residue and salt eat into paint and undercarriage parts.

Regular washes keep that buildup from setting in — our maintenance plan takes up to 40% off recurring visits. For a bigger reset, the Elite Detail adds clay bar and iron decontamination to pull out what a normal wash leaves behind.
