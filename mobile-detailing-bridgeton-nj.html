Car Care Notes for Bridgeton Drivers

Bridgeton's older neighborhoods and the streets around Bridgeton City Park are lined with mature trees — great for shade, rough on paint. Tree sap, pollen and bird droppings are all acidic, and in summer heat they can etch into clear coat within days.

If you park under trees, a Signature Detail adds a spray wax and paint sealant that makes sap and droppings wipe off instead of bonding. Already have spots baked in? Our tar & sap removal add-on and paint correction take care of the damage.
