Common questions

What does a clay bar treatment do?
Washing removes loose dirt, but paint still holds bonded contaminants you can feel as roughness. A clay bar lifts those off so the paint feels smooth and sealants or coatings bond properly.
Is the Elite worth it over the Signature?
If your carpets or headliner are dirty, there's a lingering smell, or the paint feels rough to the touch, yes. If the car is already in good shape, the Signature usually covers it — we'll tell you honestly.
