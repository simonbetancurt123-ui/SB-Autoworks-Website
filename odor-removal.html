Get Rid of the Smell for Good

Air fresheners only cover a smell for a few days. Odors from spilled drinks, food, pets, gym bags or smoke soak into carpet, seat foam and the headliner, so they keep coming back until the source is cleaned and treated.

We clean the surfaces where the smell lives, then treat them with an enzyme-based product that breaks down odor at the source. For severe odors, we use ozone treatment as part of a Disaster Detail.

Common questions

Can you remove cigarette smoke smell from a car?
Yes, but smoke is one of the toughest odors because it gets into every soft surface and the headliner. It takes a full interior cleaning plus odor treatment, so we quote smoke jobs individually.
Will the smell come back?
If the source is cleaned and treated, it shouldn't. Masking sprays wear off — that's why we treat the surfaces themselves instead.
