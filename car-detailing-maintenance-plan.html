How the Maintenance Plan Works

Pick how often you want your vehicle detailed — anywhere from weekly to every 2 months. The more often you book, the bigger your discount on your chosen package (Refresh, Signature or Elite).

Your rate is locked in for as long as you stay on schedule. Every interval has a 7-day grace window, and there's no contract and no sign-up fee. A deposit secures each appointment.

To join, your first detail is booked at regular price to bring your vehicle to baseline. Your discounted rate starts on the next visit.

Common questions

Is there a contract for the maintenance plan?
No. There's no contract and no sign-up fee. A deposit secures each appointment, and you can leave whenever you want.
Why is my first detail full price?
The first visit brings your vehicle up to a clean baseline, which takes more work. After that, each visit is maintenance — so your discounted rate starts on the second visit.
What if I miss my scheduled interval?
Every interval comes with a 7-day grace window. Stay within it and your full discount is protected. If you miss it, your rate adjusts to the next applicable tier.
