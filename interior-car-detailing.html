Common questions

Can you get stains out of cloth seats?
Most stains come out with our pre-treatment, hot steam and shampoo extraction. Some older or set-in stains can only be lightened, and we'll tell you upfront if that's the case.
Do you clean work trucks and business vehicles?
Yes. We regularly clean work truck interiors — just let us know how many vehicles and where they're parked.
